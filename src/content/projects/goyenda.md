Goyenda (গোয়েন্দা — *detective*) sells detective cases: real-feeling case files with evidence, suspects and a solution, made to be solved alone or with friends. I started it because the alternative to a night of doomscrolling shouldn't have to be nothing.

I designed and built the whole platform myself — the site, the case format, the business behind it. It's close to launch.

How it works:

- Buy a case, download and print the evidence pack, work it like an investigator. Every case is fictional.
- The solution is emailed separately, hours later — the delay depends on the case's difficulty rank.
- Built: homepage, case catalog filterable by difficulty, case detail pages with redacted document previews, checkout, and an admin dashboard for case management with file upload.
- Still to wire before launch: a Bangladesh payment aggregator (bKash / Nagad) and the scheduled solution delivery.
